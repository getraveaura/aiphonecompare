Why is the starting price often not my real bill?

A starting price is the lowest price on the company's website. Your bill depends on how they charge, how much use is included, and what extra use costs. A low start can still mean you pay for minutes, calls, or credits past the allowance. Check what's included before you compare two prices.

How do these services bill (minutes, calls, callers)?

Ready-made AI receptionists bill in three main ways: by the minute, by the call, or by the unique caller. A minute plan cares how long you talk. A call plan charges the same for a short call and a long one. A unique-caller plan, such as Goodcall, charges once per person in a month.

What does setup cost?

Some companies list a one-time setup fee, and some say there is none. We show a fee only when the pricing page lists it. If a company doesn't list a fee, we leave it blank instead of guessing $0. AgentZap lists a $499 setup fee. Dialzara's pricing page says there is no setup fee.
